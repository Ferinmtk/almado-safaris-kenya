The classic route

A typical safari, airport to airport

Most of our guests follow a loop like this. Use it as a template — we stretch or shrink every leg to fit your dates.

1
Day 1 · Arrival · Nairobi

Touchdown at JKIA

Your driver waits in arrivals, sign in hand — then it's the hotel, dinner, and a briefing on the days ahead.

2
Day 2 · To the Mara

Into the Great Rift Valley

Photo stop on the escarpment; first game drive by afternoon.

3
Day 3 · Maasai Mara

A full day among the Big Five

Dawn drive, picnic under an acacia — balloons at first light, if you dare.

4
Day 4 · Mara → Amboseli

Elephants under Kilimanjaro

Cross country for the classic shot, then a sundowner at the lodge as Kili turns pink.

5
Day 5 · Optional · The Coast

Trade dust for ocean

Unwind on Diani's white sand — or spend the day in Nairobi's markets and the giraffe centre.

✓
Final day · Departure

Back to JKIA

Return timed around traffic and your flight. Karibu tena.
